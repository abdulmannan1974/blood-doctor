# Myeloid Sarcoma of the Breast

An evidence-based educational web app on the **diagnostic approach and management
strategies** for myeloid sarcoma (granulocytic sarcoma / chloroma) of the breast — a
rare extramedullary presentation of acute myeloid leukaemia.

Part of the **Blood Doctor** platform by Dr Abdul Mannan (FRCPath FCPS).

## Contents

- **Overview** — definition, epidemiology and key teaching points
- **Presentation** — the four clinical contexts (de novo/isolated, concurrent AML,
  relapse, blast transformation of MPN/MDS/CML)
- **Diagnostic approach** — imaging, core biopsy, the myeloid IHC panel
  (MPO, CD117, CD34, CD43, CD68, lysozyme…), flow cytometry, cytogenetics/molecular,
  and bone marrow staging
- **Diagnostic pitfalls** — common misdiagnoses (lymphoma, carcinoma) and how to avoid them
- **Management strategies** — systemic AML-type chemotherapy as the cornerstone,
  allogeneic transplant, targeted/risk-adapted therapy, radiotherapy and the limited
  role of surgery
- **Prognosis** — outcomes and the evidence-quality caveat
- **References** — peer-reviewed sources (DOI / PubMed linked)

## Disclaimer

For clinical education only. The evidence base for breast myeloid sarcoma is limited to
case reports and small series with no breast-specific prospective trials. Management
decisions must be based on individual patient assessment, multidisciplinary
haemato-oncology input, and current guidelines.
