# Epidemiology & Pathogenesis

> [!abstract] Snapshot
> Rare; affects **young women** with a tendency to **bilaterality**; the breast is an uncommon MS site; biology is linked to monocytic differentiation and specific cytogenetics.

## 📊 Frequency

| Measure | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MS among all AML patients | ~**2–8%** (range 1.4–9%) | Chisholm 2019; Patkowska 2025 |
| Isolated/de novo share of MS | ~**27%** | Pileri data (via Chisholm 2019) |
| Post-allogeneic HSCT relapse that is extramedullary | **7–46%** | Patkowska 2025 |
| Breast as the MS site | **Rare** (case-report level) | breast series & cases |

> [!note] Common MS sites (for context)
> Skin, lymph nodes, bone/periosteum, soft tissue and GI tract predominate. **Breast is among the rarer locations**, which is part of why it is so often misread — see [[Differential Diagnosis & Pitfalls]].

## 👩 Breast-specific demographics

> [!tip] Pattern to recognise
> A **young woman** (cases reported from **~28–42 years**, older patients also occur) with a **painless breast mass**, occasionally **bilateral**, should prompt consideration of MS when histology is "undifferentiated".

- Reported isolated breast-MS ages include 28, 30, 33 and 42 years; bilateral disease is specifically documented.
- Bilaterality is a recognised quirk of breast MS — see [[Clinical Presentation]].

## 🔬 Pathogenesis & site tropism

> [!question] Why the breast?
> Tropism of myeloid blasts for breast/visceral sites is **incompletely understood**. Proposed and observed associations:

| Driver / marker | Association with tissue tropism |
|---|---|
| **CD56 (NCAM)** expression | Historically linked to extramedullary (breast/gonadal/gut) homing and to **t(8;21)** — *but recent data question the strength of this link* |
| **inv(16) / CBFB::MYH11** | Tendency to involve **intestine, breast, uterus** |
| **KMT2A (MLL) rearrangements** | **Monocytic** differentiation; skin/breast infiltration |
| **t(8;21) / RUNX1::RUNX1T1** | Classic overall MS association |

> [!warning] Don't over-interpret single markers
> CD56 positivity is **supportive context, not a rule**. Lineage and subtype are established by the full [[Immunohistochemistry & Immunophenotype|phenotype]] plus [[Cytogenetics & Molecular|genetics]].
