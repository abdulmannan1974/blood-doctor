# Immunohistochemistry & Immunophenotype

> [!abstract] Snapshot
> The **myeloid IHC panel** confirms lineage and is the **single most important step** in avoiding misdiagnosis. **Always include MPO.**

> [!danger] The one marker you must not omit
> **MPO (myeloperoxidase).** Its absence from the panel is the commonest reason breast MS is misread as **lymphoma** — see [[Differential Diagnosis & Pitfalls]].

## 🎨 The myeloid IHC panel

| Marker | Lineage / meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MPO** | Granulocytic | Key positive marker; **~30% of MS are MPO-negative** (don't exclude MS on MPO alone) |
| **CD68 (KP1 / PG-M1)** | Monocytic / histiocytic | **CD68-KP1 is the most commonly expressed** marker overall |
| **Lysozyme** | Monocytic | Supports monocytic differentiation |
| **CD117 (KIT)** | Blast / progenitor | Myeloid blasts |
| **CD34** | Progenitor | Variable (often + in less-differentiated cases) |
| **CD33, CD13** | Myeloid-associated | Broad myeloid antigens |
| **CD43** | Myeloid (and T) | **+ in MS**, with B-markers negative |
| **CD56 (NCAM)** | Aberrant | Monocytic/aggressive phenotype; tropism context (see [[Epidemiology & Pathogenesis]]) |
| **CD99, TdT, CD4, glycophorin A, CD61** | Variable | Subtype-dependent (erythroid/megakaryocytic markers if relevant) |

> [!success] Pattern that confirms MS
> **Myeloid markers positive** (MPO and/or CD117/CD68/lysozyme) **+ cytokeratin negative + CD20/CD3/PAX5 negative.**

> [!failure] Markers that argue *against* MS
> Strong **cytokeratin** (carcinoma) or a coherent **B-/T-cell** programme (CD20/PAX5 or CD3 with the right context) → revisit the differential.

## 🌊 Flow cytometry

> [!note] On fresh tissue (or marrow)
> - Identifies a **myeloid/monocytic blast population**: CD34, CD117, CD33, CD13, HLA-DR ± monocytic markers (CD14, CD11c, CD64).
> - **Distinguishes** myeloid from **lymphoblastic** and **mixed-phenotype** processes.
> - Captures **aberrant antigen expression** → useful later for **measurable residual disease (MRD)** tracking.

> [!tip] Workflow synergy
> IHC + flow + **[[Cytogenetics & Molecular|FISH/RQ-PCR]]** should be **routine and concurrent** — this is how an unexpected **APL** is caught before giving the wrong therapy.
