# Differential Diagnosis & Pitfalls

> [!danger] The central message
> Breast MS is **frequently misdiagnosed at first biopsy** — most often as **lymphoma** — when a myeloid IHC panel is not requested. In the breast, where **carcinoma dominates** the differential, the diagnosis is especially easy to miss. **Consider MS in any undifferentiated/"blastic" breast malignancy and add [[Immunohistochemistry & Immunophenotype|MPO]].**

## ⚖️ Differential table

| Mistaken for | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-Hodgkin lymphoma** (DLBCL/Burkitt) | Sheets of monomorphic blastic cells, high proliferation index | **Include MPO + CD43**; confirm **CD20/CD3/PAX5 negative** |
| **Poorly differentiated carcinoma** | A solid breast mass in a woman defaults to carcinoma | **Cytokeratin negative** + myeloid markers positive (MPO, CD117, lysozyme) |
| **Inflammatory / benign** (mastitis, reactive infiltrate) | Diffuse infiltration read as reactive | Recognise **blastic morphology** + confirm **clonal myeloid** phenotype on flow |
| **Other small round blue cell tumours** | Overlapping morphology | Lineage-specific IHC + [[Cytogenetics & Molecular|genetics]] |

> [!example] Distinguishing immunoprofiles
> | | MS | Lymphoma (B) | Carcinoma |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **MPO / CD117 / lysozyme** | ✅ + | ❌ | ❌ |
> | **CD43** | ✅ + | ± (T) | ❌ |
> | **CD20 / PAX5 / CD3** | ❌ | ✅ (lineage) | ❌ |
> | **Cytokeratin** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ + |

## 🪤 Specific pitfalls

> [!failure] Sampling error in collision tumours
> MS may coexist with **invasive ductal carcinoma** in the same breast lesion. A **core biopsy can capture only the carcinoma** and miss the MS — **excisional/repeat sampling** may be needed if the clinical picture doesn't fit.

> [!failure] Missing APL
> A de novo breast MS can be **APL**. Without molecular testing, the patient may receive the **wrong induction**. → always send [[Cytogenetics & Molecular|FISH/RQ-PCR]].

> [!failure] Stopping at "lymphoma"
> An MPO-omitted panel that "fits lymphoma" can divert the patient down an entirely wrong pathway and **delay AML therapy** — which worsens [[Prognosis & Outcomes|outcome]].

> [!quote] One-line safeguard
> *If a breast malignancy looks "blastic" or "undifferentiated", run a myeloid panel before you commit.*
