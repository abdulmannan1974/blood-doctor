# Prognosis & Outcomes

> [!abstract] Snapshot
> **Heterogeneous and generally guarded.** Outcome depends on **biology, clinical context, and the timeliness of systemic therapy.**

## 📈 Key figures

| Measure | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Median OS (MS overall) | **~13 months** | Al-Obaidi 2020; Antic 2012 |
| Antic isolated-MS series | Median **OS 13 mo / EFS 8 mo**; chemo+allo-HSCT > chemo alone | Antic 2012 |
| Isolated breast MS → AML if untreated | up to **~95% within 1–48 months** | Girshova 2012; Wu 2019 |

## 🔑 Predictors

> [!note] What shifts prognosis
> - **Context:** concurrent-AML MS tracks AML outcomes; **MDS/MPN transformation confers worse survival**.
> - **Genetics:** in adults, **t(8;21)-associated MS may be worse** than the leukaemia alone; high-risk/MDS-related/*TP53* do poorly. See [[Cytogenetics & Molecular]].
> - **Treatment:** **timely systemic AML therapy** ± transplant ± [[Radiotherapy & Local Control|RT]] enables durable remissions; **delay/missed diagnosis worsens outcome**.

> [!success] The optimistic half of the story
> Breast MS case reports show **durable complete remissions** with **AML-type induction + risk-adapted consolidation** (± allo-HSCT, ± local RT). The diagnosis is dangerous mainly when **missed or under-treated** — see [[Differential Diagnosis & Pitfalls]].

> [!danger] The pessimistic half
> Treated **locally only** (surgery/RT alone), isolated breast MS **progresses to AML** in the great majority. A normal marrow at diagnosis is **not reassurance** — one reported patient with a normal marrow still died ~18 months later (Shaikh & Kayani 2015).

## ⚠️ Evidence caveat

> [!warning] Read everything above in this light
> The breast-MS literature is **almost entirely single case reports and small series**. There are **no breast-specific prospective trials** and **no site-specific survival statistics**; figures are extrapolated from MS/AML data. Apply **multidisciplinary clinical judgement**.
